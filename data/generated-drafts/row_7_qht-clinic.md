Hair loss is a complex dermatological condition influenced by genetics, hormonal fluctuations, and environmental factors. For patients seeking evidence-based care, understanding the underlying biology of the hair follicle is essential. At QHT Clinic, we prioritize a thorough diagnostic approach to assess the health of your scalp and hair shafts.

A critical metric in trichology is [follicular hair density](https://qhtclinic.com/trichology-treatments), which measures the number of active hair follicles per square centimeter of scalp. This metric helps clinicians distinguish between temporary shedding and progressive thinning. By evaluating this parameter alongside scalp condition and hair shaft strength, we can develop a personalized management plan.

Our clinical philosophy focuses on non-invasive, medically supervised interventions that support scalp health. We emphasize the importance of consistent monitoring and realistic expectations. While individual responses vary, maintaining optimal scalp hygiene and addressing nutritional deficiencies can contribute to overall hair health.

If you are experiencing concerns about hair thinning, consult with a qualified dermatologist for a comprehensive evaluation. QHT Clinic offers specialized trichology services designed to provide clear, factual guidance on your hair health journey.
