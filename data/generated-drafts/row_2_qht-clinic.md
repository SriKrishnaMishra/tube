Hair loss affects millions of individuals, often impacting self-esteem and quality of life. While various non-surgical interventions exist, hair transplantation remains a definitive surgical option for those with stable pattern hair loss. This procedure involves the precise extraction of healthy hair follicles from a donor area, typically the back or sides of the scalp, and their strategic placement into thinning or balding regions.

At [QHT Clinic](https://qhtclinic.com/treatments/hair-transplant), our approach prioritizes natural aesthetics and long-term stability. The process is tailored to the patient’s specific hair density, growth pattern, and facial features. Common techniques include Follicular Unit Extraction (FUE), where individual follicular units are harvested, and Follicular Unit Transplantation (FUT), which involves removing a strip of skin. Each method has distinct advantages regarding recovery time, scarring potential, and graft yield.

It is crucial to understand that hair transplantation is not a cure for underlying medical conditions causing hair loss, such as alopecia areata or telogen effluvium. It is primarily indicated for androgenetic alopecia. Results are gradual, with initial shedding of transplanted hairs followed by new growth over several months. Patient selection is critical; candidates must have a stable donor area and realistic expectations regarding the final outcome.

Consultation with a qualified dermatologist is the first step in evaluating suitability. Factors such as age, extent of balding, and overall health are assessed to determine if surgical intervention is appropriate.
